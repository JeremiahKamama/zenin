# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Zenin serves active individual investors who need to connect portfolio context with market research before acting. Analysts and small research desks are secondary users, using the same workspace for deeper investigation, shared context, and decision review.

## Product Purpose

Zenin brings multi-asset portfolio context, market data, research, macro events, and decision workflows into one workspace. Its purpose is to help users understand what may affect their investments, make a decision with context, and retain the rationale for later review. Success is a clear, continuous workflow from market signal to documented decision.

## Positioning

One connected workspace for multi-asset research, portfolio context, and decision review.

## Brand Personality

Contextual, multi-portfolio, and journaling-oriented. The voice is composed and informative: it should make complex market context easier to inspect without overstating certainty or turning the workspace into a spectacle.

## Anti-references

Zenin must not resemble a decorative crypto dashboard. Avoid speculative visual noise, ornamental data displays, excessive glow, and dashboard patterns that make every panel compete for attention.

## Design Principles

1. Keep market context connected to the portfolio and the eventual decision record.
2. Prioritize the next useful action over an inventory of equal-weight panels.
3. Use semantic performance, risk, freshness, and warning states as information—not decoration.
4. Preserve multi-portfolio and cross-asset context without hiding it behind fragmented tools.
5. Make journaling and review a natural continuation of research, not a separate administrative task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, semantic status labels, reduced-motion support, and dark/light contrast parity. No additional accessibility requirements were specified.
