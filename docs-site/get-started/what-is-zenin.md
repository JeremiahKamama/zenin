# What Zenin is

Zenin is a **multi-asset investment intelligence workspace for individual investors**. It brings portfolio context, market data, research, macro events, and decision workflows into one place so you can understand what may affect your investments — not just track prices.

Zenin's purpose is to help you connect market context to your portfolio, make a decision with that context, and keep the rationale for later review.

> ## Disclaimer
> Zenin provides **research and decision-support tools only**. It is **not** investment, tax, legal, or financial advice. Strategy probabilities, tax estimates, and prediction-market signals are informational outputs and should be independently reviewed before any investment decision.

## What you can do

- **Home** — portfolio snapshot, performance, positions, movers, market context, saved views, alerts, and next-step handoffs.
- **Watchlist** — search, add, import, and monitor multi-asset watchlists; inspect earnings and macro context.
- **Research** — open contextual asset research from a Watchlist, Portfolio, Home, or Intelligence surface, with portfolio-aware actions.
- **Portfolio** — track holdings, valuation, performance, exposure, rebalance planning, trade records, exports, and alerts.
- **Intelligence and Analytics** — market signals, event calendars, macro regime context, research queues, and provider health.
- **Options and predictions** — options chains, calculators, strategy workflows, whale activity, and prediction-market snapshots.
- **Journal and tax** — capture trade decisions and reviews, inspect outcomes, and estimate tax scenarios from recorded activity.

## Supported assets

| Asset class | Examples | Primary Zenin surface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stocks | `AAPL`, `NVDA` | Asset / company research |
| Crypto | `BTC`, `ETH` | Asset research and crypto analytics |
| Commodities | Gold, oil, copper | Commodity research |
| ETFs | `SPY`, `IWM` | ETF research |
| FX pairs | `EUR/USD`, `USD/CHF` | Currency Research Workspace |
| Macro indicators | Country or economic indicators | Macro research and Analytics |
| Bonds | Sovereigns and fixed income | Watchlist / Portfolio context |
| Options | Equity and crypto options | Options workspace |

## Data coverage is provider-dependent

Data freshness, source coverage, and availability vary by instrument and upstream provider. Zenin surfaces **cached, stale, unavailable, and reference-only** states where the underlying data does not support a live result.

See the [Data status glossary](/use-zenin/data-status-glossary) for what each state means and where you will see it.
