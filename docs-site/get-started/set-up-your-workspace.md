# Set up your workspace

A few setup steps make Zenin useful immediately.

## Watchlist

1. Open **Watchlist**.
2. Search an asset by symbol (for example `AAPL`, `BTC`, `SPY`, `EUR/USD`).
3. Add it, or **import** a list from CSV/TSV, spreadsheet, or platform text.
4. Organize items into categorized watchlists and turn on **alerts** where the plan allows.

## Portfolio

1. Confirm your connected accounts imported on the [first sync](/get-started/first-portfolio-sync).
2. Review **holdings**, **allocation**, and **exposure**.
3. Add any **manual positions** for assets outside your connections.
4. Save a **view** so the layout persists for next time.

## Notifications

1. Open **Notifications** to see your inbox.
2. Review unread items; mark what you have handled.
3. Set **notification preferences** for the alerts you want in-app (and email where available).
4. Watch for **source-health notices** when a connection degrades.

## Your first research workflow

Make research connect to your portfolio and decisions:

1. From **Portfolio** or **Watchlist**, open an asset to launch **Research**.
2. Review company, macro, or commodity context relevant to the holding.
3. Use **compare** to weigh it against a peer.
4. Send the research into a **decision** or **journal** workflow so the rationale is captured.

This turns a price check into a documented decision you can review later.
