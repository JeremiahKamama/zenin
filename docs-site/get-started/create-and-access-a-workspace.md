# Create and access a workspace

A **workspace** is your private Zenin environment: your connected accounts, watchlists, portfolio, journal entries, and saved views. You sign in once, then work inside a workspace.

## Sign in

1. Open the Zenin app.
2. Sign in with your account email and password.
3. If your organization uses single sign-on or multi-factor authentication, complete that step when prompted.

> Multi-factor authentication and SSO availability depend on the deployed provider configuration. If a method is not offered, it is not enabled for your workspace.

## Choose or create a workspace

After sign in you land in a workspace. You can:

- **Open an existing workspace** you already belong to.
- **Create a new workspace** to start fresh with your own connections and data.
- **Switch workspaces** from the workspace switcher when you belong to more than one.

Each workspace keeps its own portfolio, watchlists, and journal — switching workspaces does not merge their data.

## Roles at a high level

Workspaces use member roles to control who can change settings and connections:

- **Owner** — full control, including billing, member management, and connected-account configuration.
- **Member** — uses the workspace, connects read-only data sources they are permitted to add, and contributes journal/research content.
- **Guest / preview** — can explore a read-only preview of the workspace without representing local preview actions as live brokerage execution.

Exact permission boundaries depend on your plan and the workspace configuration. When in doubt, ask the workspace owner.

## Guest preview limitations

Guest mode is an **explicit preview entry point**. It lets you look around without connecting anything, but:

- Preview actions are **not** live brokerage executions.
- Connected-account data shown in preview is read-only context, not your own imported positions unless you sign in.
- Some actions that require saving (journal entries, saved views) are unavailable or limited until you sign in to a workspace.
