# Analytics, intelligence, options, predictions, and tax

These surfaces help you interpret context. They are **decision-support**, not decisions.

## Analytics

Review performance, exposure, and cross-market analytics. Use it to spot patterns in your own recorded activity and holdings.

**Limitations:** analytics depend on the data you have connected and recorded. Thin or missing history limits what can be computed.

## Intelligence

Intelligence views bring together event timelines, transmission paths, affected holdings, market signals, macro regime context, and research queues.

**Limitations:** signal and event coverage vary by country, instrument, and provider. Some items are reference-only.

## Options

Explore options chains, Greeks, calculators, strategy simulators, and whale-trade views.

**Limitations:** chain, Greek, and trade-tape coverage vary by venue and provider. Strategy probabilities are model outputs, not guarantees.

## Predictions

Prediction-market snapshots (for example, Polymarket-based) show sentiment as priced by a market.

**Limitations:** prediction signals are informational. They reflect a market's pricing, not Zenin's view, and may not reflect your complete circumstances.

## Tax

Indicative tax-estimation workflows estimate scenarios from your recorded activity.

**Limitations:** tax estimates are **informational only** and may not reflect your jurisdiction, filing status, or complete circumstances. They are not tax advice.

## Decision-support framing

For every analytical surface:

- Treat outputs as **input to your judgment**, not a recommendation.
- Check the freshness state of the underlying data.
- Remember that strategy probabilities, tax estimates, and prediction signals require independent review.

## What this does / does not do

**What this does**

- Surfaces analytics, intelligence, options, prediction, and tax context in one workspace.
- Marks data limitations and freshness where coverage is partial.

**What it does not do**

- It does not give investment, tax, or financial advice.
- It does not guarantee the accuracy of model or prediction outputs.
- It does not file taxes or execute trades.

**Data source and freshness**

These surfaces combine your workspace data (holdings, trades, journal) with configured providers (market data, macro, options venues, prediction markets). Coverage and freshness vary by instrument, venue, and provider.
