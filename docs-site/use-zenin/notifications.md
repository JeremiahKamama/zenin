# Notifications

Notifications keep you informed about your portfolio, sources, and journal without polling the app.

## Inbox and unread state

- Open **Notifications** to see your inbox.
- Unread items are marked; mark them read as you handle them.
- In-app alerts surface time-sensitive items (for example, a source-health notice) where you are working.

## In-app alerts

In-app alerts cover:

- **Source health** — when a connection degrades, errors, or recovers.
- **Journal reminders** — when a detected trade is ready to be journaled (where enabled).
- **Trade executions** — when a connected account imports new fills.

## Notification preferences

Set preferences to control which alerts you receive in-app. You can narrow what generates a notification so the inbox stays useful.

## Source-health notices

When a data source fails, delays, or returns partial data, Zenin raises a **source-health notice** with the specific reason where available. These notices are evidence of a provider issue, not an application error.

## Email and browser delivery scope

- **Email**: delivery depends on production email configuration and your preferences. Some notices are in-app only.
- **Browser**: browser delivery depends on the deployed configuration and your browser permissions; it is not guaranteed in every environment.

If you expect an email and do not receive one, confirm email is enabled in preferences and that production email delivery is configured for your deployment.

## What this does / does not do

**What this does**

- Collects workspace notifications in one inbox with unread state.
- Surfaces source-health and journal/trade alerts in-app.
- Respects your notification preferences.

**What it does not do**

- It does not guarantee email or browser delivery in every environment.
- It does not retry indefinitely; source syncs follow each source's retry behavior.
- It does not send trade-execution emails unless production email is configured and enabled.

**Data source and freshness**

Notification events are generated by the backend from workspace activity and source sync results. Email delivery status depends on the configured email provider; in-app delivery is immediate within the session.
