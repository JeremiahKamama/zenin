# Zenin Theme Notes

## Current tone

- Dark-mode-first product language
- Cyan and icy blue accents already exist in the authenticated app
- Public site and app shell are visually related but not identical

## Draft alignment

The Superdesign draft uses a terminal-inspired executive workspace style:

- near-black and charcoal surfaces
- disciplined borders and separators
- restrained accent usage
- compact operator-grade typography
- premium but not decorative density

## Implementation guidance

- Reuse existing dark shell tokens where possible
- Keep accent use sparse and operational
- Avoid introducing public-site gradients or consumer-style cards into the authenticated dashboard
- Favor border, spacing, and hierarchy over glow effects
