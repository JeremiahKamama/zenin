## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This no longer reads as a generic card dashboard on its principal surfaces. Home and Watchlist have a clear command-desk hierarchy, and risk/performance color behaves as operational information. The remaining template-like cue is legacy specialist tooling: repeated bordered panels and tiny terminal labels make some calculator screens feel assembled rather than composed.

**Deterministic scan:** The scan found 16 typography warnings in `frontend/src/styles.css` (one `Inter` declaration and 15 `Geist Mono` declarations). These are style/personality warnings, not functional defects. The previous width-animation warning has been removed. No thick side-accent border findings remain.

**Visual evidence:** The live Watchlist shows the intended sequence—page intent, concise summary, then the tracked table—with filters in one secondary entry point. Browser overlay injection was not available because the browser evaluation surface is read-only; DOM and screenshot inspection were used instead.

## Overall Impression

Zenin now has a credible data-first workspace language. The biggest opportunity is to finish the migration by treating secondary metrics as open strips and decision outcomes as the only dominant surface in every specialist tool.

## What's Working

- Watchlist brings tracked positions into the first viewport and consolidates display controls behind `Filters & view`.
- Home pairs a clearly featured portfolio value with a risk rail, giving performance and risk distinct jobs in the composition.
- Semantic surfaces now distinguish healthy, risk, warning, stale, and information states without relying on side rails or color alone.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Legacy specialist tools still repeat container treatments

**Why it matters:** Equal-weight panels obscure the calculation outcome and make a decision workflow feel like a settings screen.

**Fix:** Continue converting supporting result cards into metric strips and group paired venue inputs inside one container with dividers. Reserve a full surface only for the decision verdict or primary data table.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P1] Semantic state application is not yet fully tokenized across legacy rules

**Why it matters:** Raw green/red/amber declarations can drift in theme contrast and dilute the meaning of portfolio health or risk.

**Fix:** Route remaining performance, risk, and freshness treatments through the named OKLCH semantic token layer. Keep icon plus status text with every colored state.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable colorize`

### [P2] The type system still mixes a polished workspace with terminal-density labels

**Why it matters:** Very small all-caps labels work for dense metadata but lower legibility when they become the dominant reading mode in calculator forms.

**Fix:** Keep compact labels only for supporting metadata and let form group titles and decision labels use the standard body/section scale.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`

### [P2] Font detector warnings indicate a generic typography footprint

**Why it matters:** The functional interface is strong, but widespread Inter/Geist references make the system less distinctive.

**Fix:** Establish a deliberate display/body/mono pairing, then migrate legacy local declarations to its tokens.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`
